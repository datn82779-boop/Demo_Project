# Grammar Roadmap (Global Success 6–9)

Use this roadmap to confirm which grammar structures are appropriate for the target grade and unit. When generating exercise content (sentences, questions, options, passages), keep within or below the target unit — do not borrow structures from higher units (students haven't met them yet).

If a current-unit structure would make a sentence feel forced, it's fine to skip it: naturalness matters more than ticking the grammar box.

## Global Success 6 (GS6)

- **Unit 1**: Present simple, Adverbs of frequency
- **Unit 2**: Possessive, Prepositions of place
- **Unit 3**: Present continuous
- **Unit 4**: Comparative adjectives
- **Unit 5**: Countable and uncountable nouns, Modal verbs: must/mustn't
- **Unit 6**: Should/shouldn't for advice, Some/any for amount
- **Unit 7**: Wh-question words, and, but, so
- **Unit 8**: Past simple, Imperatives
- **Unit 9**: Possessive adjectives, Possessive pronouns
- **Unit 10**: Future simple, Might for possibility
- **Unit 11**: Articles, 1st conditional
- **Unit 12**: Superlative adjectives

## Global Success 7 (GS7)

- **Unit 1**: Present simple
- **Unit 2**: Simple sentence
- **Unit 3**: Past simple
- **Unit 4**: Comparisons: like, different from, (not) as … as
- **Unit 5**: Some, a lot of, lots of
- **Unit 6**: Prepositions of time and place
- **Unit 7**: It indicating distance, Should/shouldn't
- **Unit 8**: Connection: although/though, however
- **Unit 9**: Yes/No questions
- **Unit 10**: Present continuous
- **Unit 11**: Future simple, Possessive pronouns
- **Unit 12**: Articles

## Global Success 8 (GS8)

- **Unit 1**: Verbs of liking/disliking + gerunds, Verbs of liking/disliking + to V
- **Unit 2**: Comparative forms of adverbs
- **Unit 3**: Simple sentences and compound sentences
- **Unit 4**: Yes/No and Wh- questions, Countable and uncountable nouns
- **Unit 5**: Zero article
- **Unit 6**: Future simple, 1st conditional
- **Unit 7**: Complex sentences with adverbs clauses of time
- **Unit 8**: Adverbs of frequency, Present simple for future events

- **Unit 10**: Prepositions of time and place, Possessive pronouns

- **Unit 12**: Reported speech (questions)

## Global Success 9 (GS9)

- **Unit 1**: Grammar words before to V, Phrasal verbs

- **Unit 4**: Past continuous, Wish + past simple
- **Unit 5**: Present perfect

- **Unit 8**: Relative pronouns
- **Unit 9**: Defining relative clauses

- **Unit 11**: suggest / advise / recommend + Ving / a clause with should
